How to play

  1. Aim. Hold ◀ ▶ (or drag the screen) to turn. Pick a club with ▲ ▼ or tap its name to open your full bag. Check the wind and the map.
  2. Swing. Tap SWING to start the meter, tap again to lock in your power.
  3. Impact. The cursor races back. Tap when it hits the green mark. Early slices, late hooks. A slice curves right for righties and left for lefties.
  4. Putt. Greens slope. Watch the BREAK arrow and aim uphill of it.
  5. Score. Water and out-of-bounds cost a stroke. Fewest strokes across 18 holes wins the cup.
  6. Save & jump. Your round saves after every shot. Tap the hole name (top left) to jump to any hole, or ☰ for the menu.
