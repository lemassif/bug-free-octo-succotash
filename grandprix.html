How to drive

Steer with ◀ and ▶ on the left. GAS and BRAKE are on the right. Turn on Auto gas and the car speeds up by itself, so you only steer and brake.

Brake before the corners, not in the middle of them. Going too fast into a turn makes the car slide wide.

BOOST gives you extra speed. The blue bar fills up as you drive. Save it for the long straights!

Stay on the track. Grass slows you down and the walls bounce you off.

Wait for the five red lights to go out, then GO!

Keyboard: arrows or WASD, Space for boost, C for camera, P to pause.
