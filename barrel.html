Big Bongo the gorilla grabbed Pickles the puppy and climbed to the top of the tower! Jump the barrels, dodge the fireballs, climb the ladders and save her.

☰ Arcade menu

How to play

◀ ▶ walk. ▲ ▼ climb ladders (stand at the bottom or top of one). JUMP over barrels, crates and fireballs. Every jump over one is 100 points.

Ladders with missing rungs are broken. You can climb partway up one to let a barrel roll past underneath, but it won't take you to the next floor.

Grab a hammer to smash barrels and fireballs for 9 seconds.

4 kinds of level: 🛢️ Girders: climb to Pickles while barrels roll down. 📦 Factory: conveyor belts push you and carry crates. 🛗 Lifts: ride the moving lifts and watch for bouncing springs. 🔩 Rivets: walk over all 8 yellow rivets to make Big Bongo fall!

Easy: slow barrels, 5 lives and falls don't hurt. Medium and Hard are faster, and a big fall costs a life.

Bonus points count down, so be quick. Keyboard: arrows or WASD, Space to jump, C camera, P pause, M sound.
