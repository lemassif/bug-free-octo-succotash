How to play

Tap the sky where you want a fireball. A counter-missile flies there from the closest base and explodes. Any enemy missile that touches the fireball blows up too.

Aim ahead of a falling missile, so it flies into your fireball. One fireball can catch several missiles at once!

You have 3 bases with missiles. The middle one shoots fastest. If a base is hit, it can't shoot until the next wave.

Watch for: missiles that split into 3, bombers and satellites that drop missiles, and from wave 7 smart bombs that dodge your fireballs.

After each wave you get points for every city still standing and every missile you didn't use. Points also earn bonus cities that rebuild one you lost. Lose all your cities and the game is over.

Easy has slower missiles, bigger fireballs and 15 missiles per base. Keyboard: mouse to aim and click, or arrows and Space. P pause, M sound.
