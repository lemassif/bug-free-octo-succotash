How to play

Move: left thumb on the left side. Look: drag anywhere else.

JUMP twice for a double jump. Hold JUMP in the air to blast up with your jet boosters. When the fuel runs out, keep holding to glide with your wings. Fuel fills up again on the ground.

MINE: hold it while aiming the + at a block to dig it out. It also hits monsters.

BUILD: puts the block you picked at the bottom where the + is pointing. Blocks never run out. Tap ALL BLOCKS to pick from every kind of block. Try the Build World on the level map to build anything you like!

Boat parts glow gold with a beam of light. The arrow at the top points to the nearest one. Some are buried, some are on tall pillars, some are on other islands.

BOAT: walk up to your boat and press BOAT to ride it. Find every part, then get back in your boat to finish the level.

Bosses on levels 7, 14 and 20. Watch their attacks and jump out of the way!

Keyboard: WASD move, mouse look (click first), Space jump, left click mine, right click build, 1-8 pick a block, Q all blocks, hold Space to fly, E boat, V camera, Esc pause.
