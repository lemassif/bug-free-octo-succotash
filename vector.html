How to play

Move by dragging your thumb anywhere on the left side of the screen. FIRE shoots the way your ship is pointing.

Fly over a glowing square and press BUILD to make an energy block. Fill every glowing square to power up the grid and win the level. On Easy the ship builds by itself when you hover over a square.

Each block costs 1 energy. Energy refills slowly, and shooting enemies gives you more.

Build 4 blocks in a square and a power gem appears that shoots enemies for you!

Enemies: pink Hackers eat your blocks (shoot them fast!), yellow Zappers shoot at you, and orange Bouncers zoom off the walls. On levels 4, 8 and 12 the Mother Saucer attacks: shoot its 3 glowing pods.

Keyboard: arrows or WASD to fly, Space to fire, B or Enter to build, P pause, M sound.
