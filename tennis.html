How to play

Moving. On Easy and Medium your player runs to the ball for you. On Hard you move with the joystick (or the arrow keys / WASD).

Hitting. Tap a shot button as the ball comes to you: Topspin (green) dips into the court and kicks up high, Slice (blue) floats and skids low, Lob (yellow) goes high over a player at the net. Keyboard: J, K, L.

Aiming. Hit early and the ball goes across the court, hit late and it goes down the line. Or hold the joystick left or right as you hit.

Serving. Tap where you want to serve in the box (or use the joystick), then tap a serve: Flat (fast), Slice (curves away) or Kick (jumps up high). That tosses the ball. Tap again when the power bar is in the green. Two faults in a row is a double fault.

The surfaces. Melbourne and New York are hard courts. Paris is red clay: slow, with high bounces and long sliding rallies. London is grass: fast, with low skidding bounces, so go to the net!

Scoring. Real tennis scoring: 15, 30, 40, game. At 40-40 it is deuce, and you need two points in a row. Win the set to win the match.
