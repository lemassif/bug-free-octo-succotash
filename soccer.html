How to play

Move: put your thumb anywhere on the left side and slide it. The player with the yellow ring is you. Keep the ball close and you dribble it.

PASS: tap to pass to a teammate. Point the stick where you want the ball to go. Without the ball, PASS switches to the teammate nearest the ball.

SHOOT: hold to power up, let go to shoot. Push the stick up or down to aim at a corner. Without the ball, SHOOT is a slide tackle.

Score a goal and pick a celebration: SIUUU, knee slide, airplane, backflip or dance!

Keyboard: arrows or WASD move, Space or J passes, K shoots (hold), P pauses.
