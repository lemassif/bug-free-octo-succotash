How to play

  • Eye lasers: tap or hold anywhere on the screen. The giraffe's eyes fire at that spot and lock onto nearby targets. Don't overheat them.
  • Hoof blasters: hold BLAST to fire the laser guns on the front hooves straight ahead.
  • Wings: hold FLY to flap up over the buildings, let go to glide down. Flying uses up the WINGS meter, which refills on the ground. Fly straight into flying robots and spaceships to ram them.
  • Buildings: laser or blast the buildings to demolish them for big points.
  • Turbo ramps (the glowing yellow ones) launch you at 10 times your speed with an automatic backflip and 360 ollie. You smash through anything in your way.
  • Jump over cones, fences, rocks and gaps, and launch off ramps. Tap JUMP again in the air for a kickflip, then a backflip, then a 360 ollie. Land it cleanly for points.
  • Rails: roll into a rail and you'll grind it for points every second.
  • MEGA: blasting targets, landing tricks and grabbing power cells fills the meter. Hit MEGA to transform into an unstoppable robot giraffe: invincible, twin auto-lasers, double points.
  • Robots on the ground block your way: blast them or jump them. Crash three times and the run is over.
  • Keyboard: Space jump, hold ↑ or W to fly, J blast, K mega, F eye lasers at the nearest target, mouse to aim, P pause.
